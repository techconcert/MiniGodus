MiniGodus clock preview

This runs the exact game code that goes on the clock, compiled for the browser. What you see on the 240×240 screen is what the ST7789 will show, strip for strip. Timing and WiFi are simulated.

World

World: –

Load seed shows exactly the world of that seed. New world picks a random seed and never repeats the landform and climate of the world before. The two menus force a landform or climate for the next world.

Monument and the end of the world

Phase: –

Set stage lays the monument out if needed and raises it to that stage at once. Worship day starts the gathering now (use it with stage 5); Summary shows the end-of-world screen for a finished monument; Extinction kills every villager (the summary follows within minutes). The summary stays until the next morning, then a new world starts.
